Privacy Policy for Jot

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Jot ("the app," "we," "us") is a personal note-taking and memory app for iOS. This policy explains what information Jot collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. It applies to the Jot iOS app and its backend services.

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of the rights described below, contact us at veret.evg@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Account information

Jot can be used anonymously or signed in with Google.

  • Before you sign in, Jot creates a random identifier stored securely on your device (in the iOS Keychain) so your notes can be saved and later linked to an account if you sign in.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information from Google (such as your name, email address, and profile photo) to create and identify your account.

Content you create

Jot stores the content you enter or import, including:

  • Notes/memos you write ("jots"), including any titles, dates, categories, and list items (e.g. films, books, places you're tracking).
  • Text extracted from PDF bills, invoices, and payment confirmations you import. Jot extracts the text on your device and never uploads or stores the original PDF file — only the extracted text and the details you confirm (vendor, amount, dates, etc.) are saved.
  • Questions you ask Jot ("Ask") and the answers you receive.

This content is stored as plain text — it is not end-to-end encrypted. It is protected in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest using our backend provider's standard infrastructure security, the same way a typical notes app protects your data, but Jot staff or our infrastructure providers could theoretically access it if legally compelled or required for debugging/support. We do not read your notes for any other purpose.

Usage and subscription information

  • Number of messages/actions used and your remaining credit balance.
  • Subscription status (free or Jot Pro) and renewal state.
  • Basic diagnostic information needed to operate the service (e.g. request timestamps, error logs).

Jot does not receive or store your payment card details. Purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store; subscription status is confirmed to us through our payments partner, RevenueCat (see below).

How Your Information Is Used

  • To store and sync your notes across your signed-in devices.
  • To answer questions you ask about your own notes ("Ask"). This is done by searching your notes on your device, then sending your question (and any relevant note content needed to answer it) to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate a response. See "Third-Party Services" below.
  • To extract structured details (vendor, amount, dates) from bills/invoices you import, using the same AI provider.
  • To operate the credits and subscription system (tracking usage, granting Jot Pro credits, renewing subscriptions).
  • To maintain and improve the reliability of the app (debugging, fraud prevention, abuse prevention).

We do not use your notes for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Your notes are synced to our backend (built on Convex) so they're available if you reinstall the app or sign in on a new device — this is the durable copy of your data. A working copy is also kept on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData) for fast, offline access.

Your "Ask" conversation history (your questions and Jot's answers) is kept only on your device and is not synced to our backend.

Third-Party Services

Jot relies on the following third-party services to operate. Each only receives the information needed to perform its function:

ServicePurposeWhat it receives
Google Sign-In Account authentication Your Google sign-in and basic profile info (name, email, photo)
Anthropic (Claude API) Generating answers to your questions and extracting bill details The text of your question/request and relevant note content needed to answer it, sent through our backend
Convex Backend database and sync Your notes/content, account identifiers, and credit/subscription state
RevenueCat Subscription management An anonymous purchase identifier and subscription/receipt status (no payment card details)
Apple (App Store / StoreKit) Processing subscription payments Handled entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics/tracking SDKs in Jot.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can delete individual notes, list items, or bills at any time from within the app.
  • You can sign out of Jot at any time.
  • To request deletion of your account and all associated data, or to request a copy of your data, email veret.evg@gmail.com. We will process your request within a reasonable time.

Data Retention

We retain your account and note content for as long as your account is active, so that your notes remain available to you. If you request account deletion, we will delete your stored content and account identifiers, except where we are required to retain limited records (e.g. for fraud prevention or legal compliance).

Children's Privacy

Jot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of Jot after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at veret.evg@gmail.com.
