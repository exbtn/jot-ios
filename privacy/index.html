Privacy Policy for Jot

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Jot ("the app," "we," "us") is a personal note-taking and memory app for iOS. This policy explains what information Jot collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. It applies to the Jot iOS app and its backend services.

The data controller is Yevhenii Veretennikov, a sole trader (jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza) registered in CEIDG in Poland — full registration details are in the Terms of Use.

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of the rights described below, contact us at veret.evg@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

Account information

Jot can be used anonymously or signed in with Google.

  • Before you sign in, Jot creates a random identifier stored securely on your device (in the iOS Keychain) so your notes can be saved and later linked to an account if you sign in.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive a Google identity token and your Google account ID, which we use only to create and identify your account. We do not request or store your name, email address, or profile photo.

Content you create

What happens to your content depends on which feature you use:

  • Notes/memos ("jots"), including any titles, dates, categories, and list items (e.g. films, books, places you're tracking), are synced to our backend and linked to your account identity so they're available across your signed-in devices.
  • Bills, invoices, and payment confirmations you import are processed on your device: Jot extracts the text using on-device text/OCR recognition and never uploads or stores the original PDF file. The extracted text is sent to our AI provider (see "Third-Party Services" below) to identify structured details (vendor, amount, dates). Both the extracted text and the confirmed details are stored only on your device and are not synced to our backend — unless you choose to turn a bill into a finance entry, in which case that resulting entry syncs like any other note.
  • Questions you ask Jot ("Ask") and the answers you receive are stored only on your device and are never synced to our backend.

Notes/memos synced to our backend are stored as plain text — they are not end-to-end encrypted. They are protected in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest using our backend provider's standard infrastructure security, the same way a typical notes app protects your data, but Jot staff or our infrastructure providers could theoretically access it if legally compelled or required for debugging/support. We do not read your notes for any other purpose.

Usage and subscription information

  • Number of actions used and your remaining action balance.
  • Subscription status (free or Jot Pro) and renewal state.
  • Basic diagnostic information needed to operate the service (e.g. request timestamps, error logs).

Jot does not receive or store your payment card details. Purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store; subscription status is confirmed to us through our payments partner, RevenueCat (see below).

How Your Information Is Used

  • To store and sync your notes across your signed-in devices.
  • To answer questions you ask about your own notes ("Ask"). This is done by searching your notes on your device, then sending your question (and any relevant note content needed to answer it) to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate a response. See "Third-Party Services" below.
  • To extract structured details (vendor, amount, dates) from bills/invoices you import: the text is extracted on your device, then that extracted text (not the original PDF) is sent to the same AI provider to identify these details.
  • To operate the actions and subscription system (tracking usage, granting Jot Pro actions, renewing subscriptions).
  • To maintain and improve the reliability of the app (debugging, fraud prevention, abuse prevention).

We do not use your notes for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Your notes/memos are synced to our backend (built on Convex) so they're available if you reinstall the app or sign in on a new device — this is the durable copy of your data. A working copy is also kept on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData) for fast, offline access.

Your "Ask" conversation history (your questions and Jot's answers) and your imported bills/invoices (extracted text and confirmed details) are kept only on your device and are not synced to our backend — unless you convert a bill into a finance entry, which then syncs like a regular note.

Third-Party Services

Jot relies on the following third-party services to operate. Each only receives the information needed to perform its function:

ServicePurposeWhat it receives
Google Sign-In Account authentication Your Google sign-in and basic profile info (name, email, photo)
Anthropic (Claude API) Generating answers to your questions and extracting bill details The text of your question/request and relevant note content needed to answer it, and text extracted on-device from bills/invoices you import — sent through our backend

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can delete individual notes, list items, or bills at any time from within the app.
  • You can sign out of Jot at any time.
  • To request deletion of your account and all associated data, or to request a copy of your data, email veret.evg@gmail.com. We will process your request within a reasonable time.

Data Retention

We retain your account and note content for as long as your account is active, so that your notes remain available to you. If you request account deletion, we will delete your stored content and account identifiers, except where we are required to retain limited records (e.g. for fraud prevention or legal compliance).

Children's Privacy

Jot is not directed at children. To use Jot you must be at least 13 years old, and at least the minimum age of digital consent in your country if that age is higher — in parts of the European Union and elsewhere it is 14, 15 or 16. See the Terms of Use for the full requirement.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone below the age that applies to them. If we learn that we have, we will delete the account and its data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, contact us at veret.evg@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of Jot after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
