Frequently asked questions

What is Jot?

Jot is a personal-memory app. You capture thoughts, notes, meals, bills, events and contacts in Memo mode - Jot figures out what kind of entry it is for you - and then you ask questions over everything you've saved in Ask mode.

Do I need an account?

No. Jot works anonymously from the moment you install it, using an identifier stored privately on your device. Signing in is optional and is what lets your notes sync to other devices and survive a reinstall.

What happens to my notes if I sign in later?

Everything you captured anonymously - your notes and your remaining action balance - is merged into your new account automatically. Nothing is lost.

What is an "action"?

An action is one AI request: saving a memo that needs classification, or asking a question. The free tier includes 5 actions per day. Jot Pro includes 200 actions per month with no daily cap. If a request fails or is deflected, the action is refunded automatically.

How do I manage or cancel Jot Pro?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Jot to change or cancel. Cancelling keeps Pro active until the end of the paid period. We can't cancel or refund a subscription on your behalf - refunds are requested through reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Jot Pro but the app still shows Free.

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the app while signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Subscription status is granted server-side, so it can take up to a minute to appear. If it still doesn't, email us with the Apple receipt/order ID.

Are my notes private? Are they encrypted?

Your notes are protected in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our infrastructure provider, but they are not end-to-end encrypted - they're stored as plain text so the AI can answer questions over them. Your Ask conversation history and imported bills stay on your device only. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Does Jot work offline?

Reading and searching your existing notes works offline, because they're kept in a local copy on your device. Saving a new memo and asking questions need a connection, because those go through the AI.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete individual notes any time in the app. For full account deletion, email veret.evg@gmail.com from the address on your account, or use the form below and pick "Delete my account".

Which devices are supported?

Jot is an iPhone app and requires a recent version of iOS. There is no Android or web version.

Troubleshooting

Notes aren't syncing / missing on a new device

  • Make sure you're signed in with the same Google account on both devices.
  • Check your network connection - sync is skipped while offline and retried later.
  • Force-quit the app (swipe up from the app switcher) and reopen it to trigger a fresh pull.
  • Notes captured anonymously live only on that device until you sign in.

"Out of actions" or an unexpected limit

  • The free tier resets 5 actions every day. Pro grants 200 per month on renewal.
  • Failed or deflected requests are refunded - if your balance still looks wrong, force-quit and reopen.

Voice capture isn't picking anything up

  • iOS Settings → Jot → make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are enabled.
  • Disconnect Bluetooth headsets to rule out a bad input route.
  • Silent mode does not block recording, but a call or another recording app can.

Ask gives a wrong or empty answer

  • Ask only searches what you've captured - if the note was never saved, there's nothing to find.
  • Try naming things the way you wrote them (a person's name, a vendor, a month).
  • If an answer is clearly wrong, send us the question you asked - it helps us tune retrieval.

The app crashes or hangs on launch

  • Force-quit and reopen.
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store.
  • Restart the device.
  • As a last resort, reinstall. Only do this while signed in - anonymous, unsynced notes are lost on delete.
